## Where Luna High added value

- It generalized real equipment pressure without retaining proprietary product
  identity or unsupported process values.
- It produced independently distinguishable equipment families instead of one
  overloaded WPH Skill.
- Its outputs were rich enough to become recognition, application, recovery and
  counterexample contracts rather than prose-only notes.
- It handled custody, projection, cardinality, good-output and uncertainty better
  than nominal cycle-time or GUI-state reasoning.

## Observed weaknesses

- It did not close every adjacent owner boundary on the first pass.
- It focused on Skill semantics more than generator/runtime schema consistency.
- It did not initially bind model evidence to an immutable candidate packet.
- It did not keep all materially changed lifecycle evidence current.

## Recommended role

Use Luna High as the primary high-throughput distiller and behavior executor.
Do not use the same run as sole author and release authority. Pair it with Sol
for adversarial owner/tooling/lifecycle review, deterministic validators for
mechanical invariants, and human disposition after the bounded correction limit.
This is a role separation conclusion, not a claim that one model family is
universally superior.
