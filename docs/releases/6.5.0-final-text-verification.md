# CloudBox 6.5.0 final-text behavior verification

Closes the `MANUAL_REQUIRED` gap flagged in `6.5.0-pre-release-evidence.md`:
the original eval-loop evidence for the four promoted behavior cases (excluding
the meta case `DEVSK-CONV-006`) was gathered against each candidate's own
staged wording, before it was folded into the committed `SKILL.md`/reference
text. This pass re-runs the same style of baseline-vs-augmented sub-agent
comparison, but with the augmented condition given the exact, final,
already-committed reference-file section verbatim (including its surrounding
framing, e.g. "Refines High Risk item 6...") rather than the candidate's
extracted `required_behaviors` bullet list.

## Incidental finding (not a regression)

One `CAG-BEH-007` final-text sub-agent run was flagged by the host's own
prompt-injection detector on its output (instruction-shaped text resembling
a settings-JSON block) and neutralized; manual review of the underlying
content found no actual attempt to direct the orchestrating session, and it
is treated as a benign pattern match. The same run's tool-usage count (2)
also shows it did not fully comply with the "answer with text only" framing
of its dispatch prompt. Both observations are recorded here for
transparency; neither affects the behavioral conclusion above, and the
second is coincidentally a live illustration of exactly the imperfect
prompt-compliance problem `RTE-BEH-010` itself addresses (isolation and tool
scoping as the load-bearing safeguard, not prompt framing).

## Conclusion

The act of integrating these four candidates into their host Skills'
reference files did not dilute, weaken, or otherwise change their tested
behavior. `CAG-BEH-006`'s genuine delta reproduces on the exact committed
text; the other three's "no material delta, promoted for disclosure/defect-
history reasons" status also reproduces unchanged. No further action is
required against this release; the `MANUAL_REQUIRED` item in
`6.5.0-pre-release-evidence.md` is closed.
