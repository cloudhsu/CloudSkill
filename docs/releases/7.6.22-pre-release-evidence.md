# CloudBox 7.6.22 pre-release evidence

Date: 2026-08-16 (Asia/Taipei)

## Scope and privacy boundary

This increment distills sanitized evidence from the Atlas legacy-product
rewrite set. The source scope was limited to product rewrite cards, shared
architecture notes, asset-resolution notes, release/evidence notes, and the
existing CloudBox Skill optimization case. Raw transcripts, product source
paths, credentials, URLs, commercial metrics, and unverified device claims were
not copied into Skill text or committed benchmark cases.

Five product classes were reduced to reusable boundaries: cross-platform board
game, number-generator utility, continuous action game, native touch projectile
game, and native resource/queue simulation. Marketing and monetization were
not promoted to a new Skill because the available evidence did not support a
reproducible marketing outcome contract.

After the four Skills were added, the same 21-record routing suite produced:

| Contract valid | 100% |

| Context integrity | 100% |
| Supporting-skill exact accuracy | 42.8571% |

Claude consistently identified the correct new primary Skill, but frequently
returned only that owner instead of the provisional supporting set. This is a
routing-composition limitation, recorded explicitly in lifecycle notes; it is
not hidden by mechanical contract repair and is not interpreted as a failure of
the new Skill's application behavior.

The four new application cases were each executed three times with selected
Skill context: 12/12 completed and passed the deterministic evidence-coverage
rubric, average score 97.1/100. The adjacent art-only, generic-quality, and
greenfield engine controls passed after a dedicated three-run greenfield retry;
the first full suite contained one transient greenfield runner error and is
retained as infrastructure evidence rather than erased.

The rubric checks explicit evidence boundaries, state ownership, deterministic
seams, migration/lifecycle contracts, release status layers, and unsupported
claim prevention. It is not a complete semantic proof and does not prove
device, visual, package, store, or market success.

viewport fit, store, and market validation remain `NOT RUN` or `MANUAL
